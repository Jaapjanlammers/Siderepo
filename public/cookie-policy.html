Cookie policy

VANTAGE CONTENT B.V. · Last updated: March 2026 · Audience: European Economic Area

This policy explains how vantage content (“we”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. For how we process personal data in general, see our privacy policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a site. We also use similar storage (e.g. browser local storage) to remember your cookie choices.

2. How we use cookies

Necessary. These are required for basic site functionality (e.g. remembering that you have made a choice on our cookie banner). We do not ask for consent for strictly necessary cookies under ePrivacy rules.

Marketing / analytics (Meta Pixel). If you click Accept on our banner, we may load Meta’s (Facebook) Pixel to measure advertising performance and conversions. That involves cookies or similar identifiers set by Meta. We only load this after you opt in.

3. Cookie table (overview)

Name / key Type Purpose Duration
vantage_cookie_consent_v1 (localStorage) First party Stores your choice: necessary only, or accept marketing pixel. Until you clear site data or change settings
Meta Pixel cookies (e.g. _fbp, _fbc) Third party (Meta) Advertising measurement and attribution when you have accepted. Per Meta’s policies (typically up to several months)

For the latest list of Meta cookies, see Meta’s help centre on cookies and data use.

4. Legal basis (EEA)

  • Necessary storage: legitimate interest / strictly necessary to provide the service you request (showing the site and remembering consent).
  • Marketing pixel: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent anytime via Cookie settings in the footer; new page loads will not re-initiate the pixel. Existing third-party cookies may remain until they expire or you delete them in your browser.

5. Managing cookies

Use Cookie settings in the website footer to change your preference. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, etc.).
