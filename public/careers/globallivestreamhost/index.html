As our Global Live Stream Host, you will be the high-energy personality driving our social presence across TikTok, Amazon Live, sports betting streams, and more. You won’t just present products—you will create shows, experiences, and memorable moments that viewers return to again and again.

This is your stage to build a powerful on-camera brand, connect with a global audience in real time, and shape the next generation of live digital entertainment.

Why You’ll Love This Stage

  • Broadcast From Anywhere: Work from your home studio, a content house, or a different city every month. We care about your performance and professionalism, not your office cubicle.
  • Professional Creative Support: Focus on hosting. Our in-house team of strategists, producers, and editors manage scheduling, analytics, and repurposing highlights into short-form content.
  • Global Recognition: Become the recognizable on-camera talent for a fast-scaling international brand, with viewers tuning in from across time zones and platforms.

Your Mission: Command the Live Feed

As our “Master of Ceremonies” for a new era of digital experiences, you own the room—even when that room is a global live chat and a camera lens.

Own the Spotlight

  • Lead high-octane live sessions where you are the primary entertainer, not just a presenter.
  • Maintain “main character energy” from the first second of the stream to the final sign-off—no dead air, no awkward fade-outs.
  • Create live moments that feel like appointment viewing, not background noise.

Architect Real-Time Community

  • Turn a stream of usernames into a real community by calling people out, responding in real time, and building running jokes and rituals.
  • Transform “lurkers” into active participants through questions, polls, shoutouts, and interactive segments.
  • Keep the chat feeling like a fast-moving VIP room where everyone wants to stay just a little bit longer.

Interactive Storytelling & Live Drops

  • Host product demonstrations and live segments that feel like a cross between a late-night talk show and an exclusive VIP launch party.
  • Master the art of the “Live Drop” by building anticipation, structuring reveals, and guiding viewers toward key calls-to-action.
  • Use narrative, pacing, and emotion—not scripts—to keep viewers watching and buying in real time.

Energy Reset Mastery

  • Reset the energy every few minutes so new viewers dropping in instantly understand the vibe and want to stay.
  • Re-introduce the show, the offer, and the “why now” at the right cadences without sounding repetitive to long-time viewers.
  • Read the room (and the chat) in real time, adjusting your tone, pace, and structure to keep engagement high.

The Talent We Crave

We care more about your live presence and performance instincts than about traditional CVs or degrees.

  • Exceptional On-Camera Presence

    You have professional-grade stamina and charisma. You’re comfortable “filling the air” with engaging, unscripted storytelling for extended live sessions.

  • Social Commerce Intuition

    You understand the psychology of the “Live Buy”—how urgency, trust, and excitement interact in a fast, high-energy environment.

  • Multi-Tasking Mastery

    You can read a fast-moving chat, respond to viewers by name, track performance, and continue a physical product demo simultaneously—without losing your flow.

  • Fluent English

    You have outstanding verbal communication skills and can entertain and explain clearly to a diverse international audience.
